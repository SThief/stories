# Characters as Dumas describes them (checked before drawing)

| Character | Man/woman | Age | Looks used in the drawings |
|---|---|---|---|
| d'Artagnan | man | 18 | poor young Gascon, no beard, brown hair, orange-brown country doublet (blue musketeer tabard only at the end) |
| Athos | man | ~30 | noble, calm, pale; dark shoulder-length hair, moustache; musketeer blue tabard with white cross |
| Porthos | man | ~30 | a giant, showy; gold-embroidered baldric (sword belt) across the chest, big moustache, reddish hair |
| Aramis | man | ~23 | gentle face, rosy cheeks, dark eyes, a thin straight moustache; would-be priest |
| Louis XIII | man | ~24 | long dark hair, thin moustache, small pointed beard, blue with gold, ermine-lined cape, crown |
| Cardinal Richelieu | man | ~40 | red robe and cape, red skullcap, grey pointed beard and moustache |
| Queen Anne | woman | ~24 | auburn hair, gown; the twelve diamond studs are on a ribbon bow at her shoulder |
| Constance | woman | ~25 | dark hair, the Queen's linen-maid, simple dress and cap |
